Welcome! I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science, working on continual learning, vision-language models, and novel view synthesis. My advisor is [Siddhartha Bhattacharyya](https://www.fit.edu/faculty-profiles/b/bhattacharyya-siddhartha/), and I am funded by NASA Langley Research Center. Alongside my PhD, I have worked closely with mentors from Microsoft Research, American Heart Association, Collins Aerospace. Through these collaborations, I have gained extensive experience especially in self-supervised learning, 3D/4D reconstruction, and multi-modal learning. I am currently working on compression of 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) models and plan to submit a paper on it to ECCV 2026. Also, I recently submitted my 3DGS compression algorithm to a competition hosted by Meta at ICCV 2025, and I won the 1st place for lossless 3DGS model compression.

Previously, I completed my undergraduate studies at BITS Pilani Dubai, where I finished my thesis under the guidance of [Raja Muthalaghu](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/dubai/raja-muthalagu/) and received the Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis Award. 


*Reach out by email if our interests align and you wish to collaborate!*

**Also, I’m actively seeking an internship for Summer 2026—because models aren’t the only ones that need continual learning!!**
